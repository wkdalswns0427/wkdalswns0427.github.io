## [HARMONIC Lab, Georgia Tech](https://harmoniclab.ce.gatech.edu/)
Ph.D. Student (Co-advised) &nbsp;·&nbsp; 2025 -- present &nbsp;·&nbsp; Advisor: Dr. Francis Baek

- Humanoid motion retargeting for construction and manufacturing field work.
- Retargeting objectives that preserve task structure while solving configuration for the robot's own body.
- Reinforcement learning of tracking policies for tool-use and material-handling operations.
- Worker motion transfer from video with segmented motion classification, toward a reusable library of field operations.
- Human–robot interaction (HRI) for robots operating in construction and manufacturing environments.
- LLMs as virtual personas for estimating construction-practitioner perceptions of robotic automation.
- See [Humanoid Motion Retargeting](/portfolio/humanoid-retargeting/), [Worker Motion Transfer from Video](/portfolio/motion-from-video/) and [Publications](/publications/).
